# Back Design Rules

## Canvas

The back must use the same orientation as the front.

Portrait:

```text
1024 × 1536
```

Landscape:

```text
1536 × 1024
```

## Background

Use warm ivory / light cream paper with a subtle texture.

Avoid dark antique-paper effects, coffee stains, and heavy aging.

## Visual-memory illustration

Do not fade the entire front photograph.

Instead, select 1–3 visually memorable elements from the source/front, such as:

- tower
- dome
- church façade
- bridge
- canal
- coastline
- sculpture
- shell
- skyline
- distinctive roofline
- animal
- concert venue
- architectural arch

Render them as:

- fine pencil / engraving line work
- muted blue-gray
- delicate
- low saturation
- softly fading into the page

The illustration should normally anchor toward the lower-left.

## Postal layout

Top-left:

- exactly 6 separate square postcode boxes

Top-right:

- exactly 1 empty dotted/dashed rectangular stamp frame
- no text inside

Right / lower-right:

- exactly 4 horizontal address lines

Do not include:

- a center vertical divider
- city/country labels
- decorative postage text

## Back QA

- [ ] same orientation as front
- [ ] warm ivory paper
- [ ] illustration derived from source/front
- [ ] illustration is not a full faded copy
- [ ] exactly 6 postcode boxes
- [ ] exactly 1 empty stamp frame
- [ ] exactly 4 address lines
- [ ] no center divider
- [ ] no city/country label
